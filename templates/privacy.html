Privacy Policy

SmartForward respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on SmartForward, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address
  • WhatsApp phone number
  • Gmail app password (used only for email access)

This information is used solely to provide the SmartForward email-to-WhatsApp forwarding service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to operate the SmartForward service. Specifically:

  • To connect your email account
  • To monitor incoming emails
  • To forward email notifications to your WhatsApp number

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data Security

SmartForward uses encrypted storage for sensitive information such as app passwords. We take reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

4. Email Access

SmartForward only accesses your email inbox to detect new messages and forward notifications to WhatsApp. We do not read or store email content permanently.

5. Account Deletion

Users can delete their account at any time from the dashboard. When an account is deleted, all associated data is permanently removed from our system.

6. Third-Party Services

SmartForward uses third-party services such as email providers and WhatsApp messaging APIs to deliver notifications. These services may have their own privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
