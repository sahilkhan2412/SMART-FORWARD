Data Deletion Instructions

SmartForward respects your privacy and gives users full control over their data. This page explains how users can request deletion of their personal information from our system.

1. Delete Your Account from Dashboard

Users can delete their SmartForward account directly from the user dashboard. When you click the "Delete My Account" button, your account and all associated data will be permanently removed from our database.

2. What Data Is Deleted

When an account is deleted, the following information is permanently removed:

  • Email address
  • WhatsApp phone number
  • Account configuration
  • Email monitoring settings

SmartForward does not retain deleted user data after account removal.

3. Manual Data Deletion Request

If you are unable to access your account and want your data removed, you can request manual deletion by contacting us.

4. Processing Time
